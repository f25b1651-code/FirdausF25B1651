Thermal Processing: Microbial Decay

Scenario: Monitoring the reduction of Salmonella during the heat treatment of locally produced bottled chili sauce. We use the derivative to find the instantaneous "kill rate" to prevent over-cooking.

Microbial Count: $$f(t) = N_0 \cdot e^{-kt}$$ Reduction Rate (Derivative): $$f'(t) = -k \cdot N_0 \cdot e^{-kt}$$ Where $N_0=1,000,000$ CFU/g (Initial Load), $k=0.15$ (Rate Constant), and $t$ is time in minutes.
